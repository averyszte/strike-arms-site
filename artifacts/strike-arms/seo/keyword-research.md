# Strike Arms — Keyword Research (SEO Workflow source)

**Method & data-confidence note.** This document synthesises four keyword-cluster sections (transactional-commercial, informational-legal, local-Dublin, and brand) into a single prioritised source for the Strike Arms SEO workflow. Every search volume here is an **ESTIMATE** derived from airsoft-SEO domain expertise for the Republic of Ireland market (google.ie), not measured data — no paid keyword source (Google Keyword Planner, DataForSEO, Ahrefs, or Search Console) is connected to this project. Irish airsoft is a small-but-high-intent niche, so absolute volumes are modest (most terms sit in the low tens-to-hundreds per month) and difficulty is driven mainly by UK shops that ship to Ireland (Land Warrior, Patrol Base, Bespoke Airsoft, Fire-Support) rather than raw competition. Treat all ranges as directional and verify against a live tool before allocating spend. Legal-cluster pages must frame every point as a user question and never assert Irish airsoft/firearms law as fact — mark all legal copy "verify with primary source / Alan". Priorities favour intent quality and margin over volume: repeat-purchase consumables, in-country stock/shipping advantages, the physical Dublin shop, and in-house services are Strike Arms' structural edges over cross-border UK competitors.

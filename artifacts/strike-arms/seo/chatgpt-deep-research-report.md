**Content/SERP teardown:**  We reviewed ~15 representative Irish queries (product types, local and informational).  Across these, results often mix domestic shops and UK/EU retailers.  For example:

- **“airsoft shop dublin”** – Top results are Google My Business listings and directory sites (e.g. Waze map entry), sometimes HobbyAirsoft and AirsoftEire. No dedicated guide page exists. Business-profile and local snippets rank. (Winning types: local business listing.)
- **“airsoft guns ireland”** – Dominated by global retailers’ product pages (e.g. Ubuy.ie, Amazon and Polish sites).  A GSE listing (“Airsoft Gun Ireland – GSE Airsoft Dublin”) appears, but no Irish retailer page specifically.
- **“is airsoft legal in ireland”** – Mainly forums (Reddit) and retailer blogs (HobbyAirsoft FAQ, RedWolf guide). No official or local-guide page ranks. (Winning: forum/blog.)
- **“where to play airsoft ireland”** – Forums (Reddit threads about fields) and club sites (FingalAirsoft.com) appear. Some retailer blogs mention field guides (Waterford Airsoft blog). No consolidated Irish map/list.
- **“best airsoft gun beginners”** – Mostly generic “best of” articles (US/UK sites, YouTube reviews). Very little IE-specific content.
- **“AEG vs GBBR”** – Generic airsoft guides (forums, global blogs) outrank any Irish content. 
- **Brand/model searches (e.g. “Tokyo Marui Dublin”)** – Yield global sellers (Amazon, eBay, big UK shops), occasionally Irish store if they carry it.
- **“BB weight for airsoft”** or “0.20g vs 0.30g” – US/UK forums and blogs.  

In summary, Irish SEO leaderboards are fragmented: product/category terms are won by broad e‑tailers, local queries by business directories/GBP, and informational by generic sources.  No single site (even abroad) owns *all* Irish-specific queries, leaving content gaps especially for local guides and FAQ.

**Content gaps/Opportunities:** In Ireland we found no *strong* locally-focused pages on:
- **Legal advice** (only retailer FAQs and non-official posts exist) – opportunity for thorough “Airsoft Law in Ireland” page (pending legal confirmation).
- **Where to play/field guides** (few field sites have SEO-optimised content; no national roundup) – clear candidate for `/where-to-play`.
- **Beginner guides** (most searchers find generic US/UK advice) – a localized “beginner’s first purchase” guide would attract queries like *“first airsoft gun Ireland”*.
- **Best-of or comparison lists** (e.g. “best airsoft accessories Ireland”) – likely no IE-specific article; could capture local long-tail traffic.
- **Community/Events** – no comprehensive “Irish airsoft events calendar” or club directory page on retailers’ sites.
- **Technical reference (Glossary)** – minimal in-depth content on terms (GBBR, HPA, etc), aside from unsourced retailer blogs; good for `/glossary` or detailed guides.
- **Brand hubs** – many searches just find store listings; building dedicated pages for each brand stocked (with Irish advice, e.g. “Krytac guns we sell and recommend”) fills a gap.

On **Structured data / Local SEO**:  
All major Irish stores use Google Business Profiles (HobbyAirsoft, AirsoftEire, etc.) with reviews. HobbyAirsoft has ~50 reviews (3.9★), AirsoftEire ~40 reviews (4.5★), boosting local queries. Few UK retailers do (only those with NI branches).  No competitor seems to implement rich FAQ schema or the like on product pages (mostly static HTML stores), so adding FAQ/HowTo markup on guides could stand out.  None of the Irish shops use blog schema or video markup; owning video content (e.g. store tour or demo) might create SERP snippets.

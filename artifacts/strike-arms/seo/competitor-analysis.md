### 3) Content gaps — Ireland-specific monopoly openings

Ranked by opportunity for Strike Arms (Dublin, in-house repairs/upgrades):

1. **IE legal cluster done properly.** Rivals (justbbguns, redwolf, waterfordairsoft) state Irish law assertively as fact. Per house rules, Strike Arms should differentiate by framing every point as a question or "verify with a primary source or Alan," and by pointing readers to the relevant primary legislation as what to check for themselves rather than making any legal assertion of our own — a more trustworthy, E-E-A-T-defensible `/airsoft-law/` hub. Do not state any figure or rule as settled fact.
2. **UK-import-friction page.** UK shops (Patrol Base, Land Warrior) can ship to ROI but only with post-Brexit friction — slower road/sea transit, possible VAT/customs handling, higher free-ship thresholds. A "Buying airsoft from the UK vs Ireland — VAT, customs, delays" page converts that friction into a reason to buy local. No IE shop owns this.
3. **Beginner buyer's guide, IE-local.** Queries #11–12 are won by non-Irish sites. A "Your first airsoft gun in Ireland" pillar (AEG-first, budget, legal considerations to verify, kit checklist, where to play) with internal links to category + services is a clear monopoly opening.
4. **/where-to-play Dublin/Leinster directory.** Only airsofteire and clubs/venues hold this. A shop-hosted, regularly-updated site directory captures top-of-funnel beginners and earns local links.
5. **Dedicated services pages** (gearbox rebuilds, MOSFET installs, chrono, hop-up tuning, custom/DMR builds). Competitors only mention repairs; nobody ranks a real service page. Strike Arms' in-house workshop is a genuine differentiator with near-zero local competition.
6. **Consumables/parts explainers** (green gas vs CO2, bio BB weights, battery/MOSFET basics) — informational queries IE shops ignore, feeding internal links to the products.
7. **Brand hub + comparison content** (G&G vs Specna vs Krytac, "best beginner AEG in Ireland"). IE shops list brands but publish no reviews/comparisons — an easy authority win.

### 4) Local-SEO, structured-data and review-signal patterns

- **Local pack / GBP.** Hobby Airsoft and GSE lean on Dublin GBP listings, opening hours and NAP consistency. Strike Arms should ensure a complete Google Business Profile, consistent NAP across footer + schema, and a `/airsoft-shop-dublin` local landing page.
- **Structured data.** Expect these on strong pages (verify each competitor's actual markup): `LocalBusiness`/`Store` with address, openingHoursSpecification, geo; `Product` + `Offer` (price, availability, priceCurrency EUR) on product pages; `BreadcrumbList` on category paths; `FAQPage` on legal/beginner articles; `ItemList` on roundup/where-to-play pages. Strike Arms gaps to fill: `Product`/`Offer`, `LocalBusiness`, `FAQPage`, `BreadcrumbList`. Only mark up review data you genuinely have — do not fabricate `aggregateRating`.
- **Review signals.** Special Ops leans on TripAdvisor (venue); shops rely on Google reviews and social proof (TikTok/YouTube build/repair content doubles as E-E-A-T). Strike Arms' opening: surface genuine Google reviews, and use the in-house workshop for authentic repair/build content (video + author bylines from "Alan") to build topical trust.
- **Cross-border trust messaging.** TSAirsoft wins NI+ROI intent with an explicit "no import fees/taxes" message. Strike Arms ships ROI + NI — an equivalent prominent "ships across Ireland" trust block (and a dedicated shipping page) is worth replicating. Keep messaging to what Strike Arms can actually guarantee.
- **Freshness/updated-date signals.** Justbbguns dates its legal guide "2026". Add visible "last reviewed" dates on legal/beginner pages.

**Uncertainties to verify before publishing:** all authority/DR/traffic estimates; current Patrol Base / Land Warrior ROI shipping policies; each competitor's live schema; exact SERP positions (US-based search tooling was used — confirm on google.ie); what is actually live at strikearms.ie today. All Irish-law points above are competitor claims, not legal advice — frame Strike Arms' own pages as questions and verify against primary sources or Alan.

# ChatGPT research prompt — Strike Arms homepage

Paste the block below into ChatGPT (ideally a model with web browsing enabled) to run an
independent research pass and cross-check our synthesis. It's written to be self-contained.

---

You are a senior ecommerce UX and conversion strategist. I run **Strike Arms**, a small,
specialist **airsoft shop in Dublin, Ireland**. Profile:

- One physical **walk-in shop** — the **only walk-in airsoft store in north Dublin**, and the
  **oldest airsoft shop in Dublin** (17 years).
- Owner **Alan** is a 17-year veteran who gives honest, expert advice and does **in-house
  repairs/servicing**.
- Small curated catalogue (not a mega-store): rifles (AEG), pistols (gas/GBB), snipers, BBs &
  gas, batteries, tactical gear/loadout, upgrades & parts.
- Brand feel: **dark, industrial, tactical — premium but approachable, not "gamer RGB".**
- Selling online + in store. No loyalty programme, no personalisation engine, no ad platform.
- Ireland-specific: **do not state Irish airsoft/firearms law as fact** — treat legal points
  as question-framed guidance to verify, and note that UK concepts (UKARA, VCRA) do **not**
  apply here.

**I need a homepage layout plan.** Please deliver:

1. **A recommended homepage section order**, top to bottom, for a *small specialist with a
   physical shop* (explicitly NOT a big-box/marketplace layout). One line of rationale per
   section, grounded in evidence (cite Baymard / NN/g / real brand examples where you can).

2. **A "cut list"** — the homepage section types that are usually low-value filler and should
   be removed or merged (e.g. generic "why buy from us" blocks, rotating carousels, walls of
   text, repeated trust strips, urgency banners). Explain why each is filler.

3. **Trust-signal strategy** — which trust signals matter most for a *local, high-trust,
   regulated hobby* purchase, and exactly where to place them. Weigh a real named owner + a
   physical shop + longevity + in-house repairs vs. generic badges.

4. **How to make a SMALL catalogue feel premium** — concrete techniques (photography,
   curation, editorial, restraint), referencing brands like Death Wish Coffee, Fellow, Ridge,
   Arc'teryx, Filson.

5. **Airsoft/tactical-specific patterns** — study homepages of Patrol Base, Land Warrior
   Airsoft, Evike, Airsoft GI, Taiwangun, RifleGear. What does this category do that general
   ecommerce doesn't (restock alerts, "we're players too", beginner onboarding, brand walls,
   categorise by type vs playstyle)? What do these sites do **badly** (clutter!) that I should
   avoid?

6. **An image/asset system** — folder structure, formats (SVG vs WebP), consistent
   dimensions/aspect ratios per slot (brand logos, category tiles, play-style scenes, shop
   photos), and whether labels/tags should be baked into images or kept as editable HTML/CSS.

7. **Mobile-first ordering notes** — how the section order and above-the-fold budget change on
   mobile.

Be specific and opinionated, not generic. Prefer concrete section-by-section recommendations
over principles. Where you rely on a specific site or study, name it.

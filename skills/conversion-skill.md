# Conversion Skill

## Core Philosophy
Websites should generate trust and action.

## CTA Standards
- Clear CTA above the fold
- Natural CTA wording
- Avoid vague buttons
- Reduce friction in forms

## Trust Signals
- Reviews/testimonials
- Real business imagery
- Clear contact details
- Consistent branding

## UX
- Fast-loading pages
- Mobile-first layouts
- Clear navigation
- Easy inquiry process

## Avoid
- Overwhelming users
- Too many CTAs
- Long unnecessary forms
- Generic marketing buzzwords

Paste the sign-in emails into Supabase

  1. Open Supabase > Authentication > Emails.
  2. For each email below, click its name in Supabase.
  3. Copy the subject here, paste it into Subject heading. Copy the body, select everything in the Supabase body box, paste over it.
  4. Click Save, then move to the next one.
